## Global Constraints

Binding on every task. A violation is a failed review.

1. **Metric-only.** Macros are per 100 g or per unit, driven by `unit_type`
   (`'gram' | 'unit'`); the divisor rule lives in `core/macros.ts` — do not
   re-derive it.
2. **`null` sub-macro = UNKNOWN, never 0.** `sugar_g_per_unit`,
   `saturated_fat_g_per_unit` and the new `salt_g_per_unit` are nullable.
   `ingredientToForm` maps `null` → blank string (not `"0"`), and the OFF client
   preserves `null` when OFF has no value. Turning an unknown into a zero is a
   data-integrity bug, not a formatting choice.
3. **`src/core/macros.ts` and `src/core/subMacros.ts` are FROZEN** (~30
   importers, dual-runtime Vite + Deno edge).
4. **Do not break the three redesigned consumers.** `IngredientAutocomplete` and
   `AddIngredientSheet` (recipe editor, wave 5) and `AddToDaySheet` (Diario, wave
   2) all call `useLocalIngredientSearch(query, 12, enabled)` and read
   `id`, `ingredientDisplayName(ing, lang)`, `brand`, `kcal_per_unit`,
   `unit_type`. The Diario also reaches into the **`ingredientes` i18n namespace**
   for `list.perUnit` / `list.per100g` — do not rename those keys. The
   `['ingredients', …]` query keys must not be renamed.
5. **`hide_owned_ingredient` is a ref drop, not a delete** (R-25); the FK from
   `recipe_ingredients` is `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, so a pool row can never be
   removed. The UI must not promise deletion — it removes from *your library*.
6. **Reuse the shared kit**: `PageShell`, `PaginationBar` + `usePagination`
   (shared with Recetas and Ejercicios — do not fork), `EmptyState`,
   `ResponsiveDialog`, the shadcn primitives in `src/components/ui/`.
7. Every new user-facing string in ES **and** EN. No hardcoded copy.
8. Mobile-first (mobile artboard = base, web artboard = `md:`+). Light **and**
   dark theme. **The canvas's ingredient screens hardcode raw oklch** for the OFF
   amber, the base green and the macro-split pastels — they are NOT tokens. Mint
   proper tokens with dark-mode values; pasting the literals ships a light-mode
   badge onto a dark surface.
9. No new dependencies. No AI/Claude attribution (plain conventional commits).
10. **jsdom applies no CSS.** `hidden`, `md:`, `overflow` and portals are
    invisible to component tests — wave 5 shipped a dropdown clipped to
    invisibility with a fully green suite. A real-browser pass is required.

## Task 1 — Salt: schema, OFF import, form

**Goal:** `ingredients.salt_g_per_unit` exists end to end.

**Migration** (`supabase/migrations/<ts>_r33_ingredient_salt.sql`):

```sql
alter table public.ingredients
  add column salt_g_per_unit numeric(6,2)
    check (salt_g_per_unit is null or salt_g_per_unit >= 0);
```

Mirror `20260525120000_u1_sub_macros.sql` — that is the precedent for a nullable
sub-macro. **Column-only: there is no `save_ingredient` RPC** (ingredients are
written by direct table writes under RLS: `createManualIngredient`,
`importIngredientFromOFF`, `updateIngredient`), so nothing is dropped or
recreated and RLS is untouched.

Then:
- Regenerate `src/types/database.ts` with the CLI. **`docs/conventions.md` (D-A8)
  documents a post-generation patch** — the generator cannot infer RPC-arg
  nullability, so a `| null` block must be re-applied after any regen. Check
  `git diff` and confirm the only change is your column plus that patch.
- `src/lib/openfoodfacts.ts`: map OFF's `salt_100g` into the product,
  **preserving `null`** when OFF has no value (`mapOFFNutriments` already does
  this for sugar and satFat — follow it exactly).
- `src/features/ingredients/api.ts`: carry salt through `createManualIngredient`,
  `importIngredientFromOFF` and `updateIngredient`.
- `src/features/ingredients/components/IngredientFormFields.tsx` +
  `schema.ts`: a salt `NumberField` (optional, `>= 0`), `ingredientToForm` maps
  `null` → blank, `parseForm` maps blank → `null`. The existing editor keeps
  working — PR-B redesigns it; you are only making the field exist.

**Tier-3 (pgTAP):** the check constraint rejects a negative and accepts a
positive value and `null`; RLS on `ingredients` unchanged.
**Tier-1:** the OFF mapping preserves a missing salt as `null` (not 0), and
carries a present one; `parseForm`/`ingredientToForm` round-trip blank ↔ `null`.

**Out of scope:** aggregating salt into recipe/day totals. Salt is an
ingredient-level fact this wave — `core/subMacros.ts` stays frozen and untouched.

## Task 2 — The Ingredientes list, redesigned

**Goal:** `/recipes/ingredients` matches the canvas, mobile and web.

Current page: `src/pages/IngredientesPage.tsx` (renders `RecipesTabs`,
`IngredientList`, and the `IngredientDialog` modal inline).
Canvas: `IngredientesWebV2` (`ingredientes-web.jsx`) and `IngredientesMobile`
(`ingredientes-mobile.jsx`). Read both.

**Web:** header (search field, "código de barras", "nuevo ingrediente"), filter
chips with counts, and the numeric table — `Ingrediente | kcal | P | C | G |
fibra | Origen`, sunken uppercase header row, tabular numerals. The canvas also
draws a permanent 360px OpenFoodFacts side panel; **that panel belongs to PR-B's
search/create flows** — do not build it here, and say in your report that you
left the space for it.

**Mobile:** search row + camera button, the **scan banner** (accent-tinted card
— it navigates to the scanner, which PR-B builds; wire it to the route and let
it 404 until then, or stub it and say so), then rows: name + verified check,
brand, the **macro triad with dots** (P/C/G), kcal, source badge. No filter chips
on mobile in the canvas.

**Source badge — one component, not four.** The canvas has four disagreeing
implementations (`O.F.F.` vs `OpenFoodFacts`, base green vs grey, pill vs
rounded-rect). Build **one** `IngredientSourceBadge` and use it everywhere.
`source` has **four** DB values: `manual | openfoodfacts | bedca | system`. Map
`system` **and** `bedca` to the "base" badge — today `bedca` silently falls
through to "Manual", which is a mislabel waiting to happen. Make the mapping
exhaustive.

**Verified check.** `is_verified` exists and already sorts search results. Render
the badge (read-only). **There is no toggle** — verifying is a claim about a
shared-pool row someone else may own, and no RLS/RPC governs it (spec §4).

**Filter chips + counts** (mi biblioteca / verificadas / por unidad / base /
mías). The counts need real numbers — decide how you get them without
N+1 queries, and say what you chose.

**Pagination, empty state, skeleton — the canvas designed NONE of the three.**
Build them: reuse `PaginationBar` + `usePagination` (do not fork), and build the
empty state from the canvas's vocabulary (as Recetas did — see
`src/components/ui/EmptyState.tsx`). Cover both "your library is empty" and "no
results for this filter".

**Tier-2:** rows render with their macros and source badge; the filter chips
narrow the list; the empty state appears when nothing matches. Mock the data
hook (a `.test.tsx` that lets a component reach the supabase client passes
locally and **fails in CI**).

## Task 3 — Full-screen ingredient search (D-F24)

**Goal:** the deferred full-screen search finally lands, on the ingredient list.

The canvas has **no ingredient search artboard**. The pattern is established
twice — `RecetaBuscarMobile` (`src/recetas-buscar-mobile.jsx`) and
`DiarioBuscarMobile` (`src/diario-add-mobile.jsx`). Read `RecetaBuscarMobile`:
active field with an accent ring + a clear button, a `Cancelar` text action,
results with **the matched substring highlighted**, and a **pinned footer escape
hatch**. Here the escape hatch is "crear un ingrediente nuevo" / "escanear el
código" (both route to PR-B's screens).

The match-highlighter (`HiName` in the canvas) appears three times there. **Port
it once as a shared component** and unit-test it — including the cases that break
naive implementations: a match at the start, no match, a query with regex
metacharacters (`(`, `.`, `*`), and case/accent differences (the repo already has
`normalizeText` in `features/recipes/recipeFilter.ts` — reuse the normalisation,
do not write a second one).

Search hits the **whole pool**, not just your library — that is what
`useLocalIngredientSearch` already does (and what `AddIngredientSheet` and the
Diario rely on). Do not change its semantics.

**Tier-1:** the highlighter (the four cases above).
**Tier-2:** typing narrows results; the highlight wraps the matched substring;
the escape hatch is present when there are no results.
